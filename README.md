# 🎵 VibeStream – Music Streaming App UI/UX Design

VibeStream is a modern music streaming app UI/UX design created in Figma. 
The project focuses on creating a visually appealing, user-friendly, and interactive music listening experience with a dark-themed interface and vibrant accent colors.

## 📱 Project Overview

VibeStream is designed to provide users with an easy and engaging way to discover, play, and manage music.

The design includes a complete user flow from browsing music to playing songs and viewing lyrics.

## ✨ Features

- 🎧 Modern music streaming interface
- 🔍 Search for songs, artists, and playlists
- 🎵 Music categories such as Bollywood, Hip-Hop, and Romantic
- ⭐ Featured music section
- 🕐 Recently Played section
- ❤️ Like/Favourite songs
- 🎶 Music discovery through song cards
- ▶️ Mini music player
- 🎼 Full Now Playing screen
- ⏯️ Play, pause, previous and next controls
- 🔀 Shuffle and repeat controls
- 📊 Music progress bar
- 🎤 Lyrics section
- 🏠 Bottom navigation
- 👤 User profile section
- 📚 Library section

## 🎨 Design Highlights

- Dark-themed interface for a modern music-app feel
- Vibrant yellow/orange accent color for important actions
- Rounded cards and buttons
- Large album artwork
- Clear typography and visual hierarchy
- Consistent spacing and component design
- Interactive prototype flow

## 📲 Main Screens

### Home Screen
The home screen provides:
- Personalized greeting
- Search bar
- Music categories
- Featured song
- Recently played songs
- Music discovery content

### Music Discovery
Users can browse songs through visually rich music cards containing album artwork, song titles, and artist names.

### Mini Player
A persistent mini-player allows users to control the currently playing song while continuing to browse the application.

### Now Playing
The Now Playing screen provides:
- Large album artwork
- Song and artist information
- Favourite option
- Progress bar
- Playback controls
- Shuffle and repeat options

### Lyrics
The lyrics screen allows users to follow the lyrics while listening to the song.

## 🎯 Design Goals

The main goals of the project were:

1. Create a visually attractive music streaming interface.
2. Provide simple and intuitive navigation.
3. Maintain consistency across all screens.
4. Make music discovery engaging.
5. Create a smooth transition between browsing and listening.
6. Design a complete mobile music streaming experience.
